Recognized Disease Conditions

Healthy Nut

Normal fruit development with unblemished surface.

Mahali / Koleroga

Water-soaked lesions causing heavy premature nut drop.

Anthracnose

Circular sunken dark necrotic spots on tender green nuts.

Fruit Split

Cracking due to severe soil moisture imbalances.

Bacterial Blotch

Bacterial water soaking and dark oily rot patches.
